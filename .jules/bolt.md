## 2026-07-24 - [Google Business Profile Review Stats Multi-Tenant TTL Caching]
**Learning:** Google My Business API reviews and metrics endpoints are highly fragmented, slow, and expensive. Querying day-by-day aggregates (e.g., stats) requires sequential multi-page cursor-based pagination over the internet. Performing these loops on every LLM agent/tool request causes huge latency spikes and risks hitting rate limits. Adding a simple, multi-tenant memory cache keyed by `locationName` with a 5-minute TTL reduces subsequential call time from hundreds of milliseconds (or seconds) to under 1ms, while preserving data freshness and multi-tenant isolation.
**Action:** Always check for opportunities to cache read-heavy aggregate stats computed from external APIs, and make sure to invalidate the cache whenever a state-mutating operation (like `postReply` or `deleteReply`) is executed for that tenant.
